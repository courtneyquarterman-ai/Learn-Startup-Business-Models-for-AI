# Guide for the real reviewer session

The reviewer should be an instructor or an advanced quantum student. This session is still pending. This guide is a script, not a record of completed testing.

## Invitation you can send yourself
Would you be willing to spend about 10 minutes trying my Hybrid Skills Verifier prototype? It inspects saved notebook evidence, uses AI to suggest scores and follow-up questions, and records your own review. I want to learn whether the report and the AI suggestions are useful and what is missing. I will take brief notes with your permission. This is a class prototype and does not award a credential.

## Setup
1. Share the repository link, or sit together at the same computer.
2. Confirm the reviewer can run Python 3 and open HTML reports.
3. Write your own explanation using the draft as a starting point; save it as explanation.txt in the project folder.
4. Run the AI suggest command beforehand, but do not show the AI suggestions until the reviewer has written down their own three scores. Their first scores should be independent, so you can measure agreement and whether the AI changed their mind.

## Facilitator script
Ask permission to take notes. Explain that the prototype is being tested, not the reviewer. Start a timer. Ask them to follow the five tasks in docs/user-test.md, thinking aloud if comfortable. Avoid directing them to the answers; record any help you provide.

Afterward ask:
- What did the evidence let you judge?
- What could you not judge?
- What evidence would you need next?
- How useful was this report on a scale of 1 to 5, and why?
- How useful were the AI suggestions and follow-up questions, 1 to 5? Did you change any score after seeing them?
- Would you trust the AI quotes and flags? What would make you trust them more?

Record actual task times and whether the participant completed each task independently. Keep direct quotes separate from your interpretation. Avoid storing unnecessary personal information.

## Send back these notes for the final revision
Participant role and date; permission to take notes; completion/time/help for each task; usefulness rating and reason; confusing parts; missing evidence; suggested changes; any real reviewer scores before and after seeing AI suggestions; and the decision.

The PRD threshold is locating the comparison and explaining the limitations within five minutes, plus usefulness of at least 4/5. Report the observed outcome even if that threshold is missed. One participant is initial feedback, not proof of broad demand.
